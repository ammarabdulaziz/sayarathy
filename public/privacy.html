Sayarathy

Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Sayarathy is a browser application operated by Ammar Abdul Aziz for keeping car records in a JSON file in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

When you connect Google Drive, Google asks you to authorize the drive.file permission. This lets Sayarathy create, read, update, list, and delete files created by or explicitly opened with the app. It does not grant access to every file in your Drive. The current application lists only files it creates and marks as Sayarathy data.

Where your data goes

Your JSON content and file metadata are transmitted directly between your browser and Google Drive over HTTPS. Files remain in your Google account, subject to your Drive permissions. Sayarathy does not send your car records to an operator-owned backend, database, analytics service, advertising service, or AI service.

Browser storage and authorization

The app holds your Google access token, loaded JSON, unsaved edits, and activity log in browser memory. It stores the public OAuth client ID in localStorage for convenience. Tokens are not deliberately saved to localStorage. Reloading or closing the page removes in-memory state; clearing browser data removes locally saved configuration but does not delete successfully saved Drive files.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts the application and may process ordinary web request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy policies. Google Identity Services provides authorization, and Google Drive stores your files; those services process authorization and API requests under Google's privacy policies. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

Retention, deletion, and control

Your Drive files remain until you delete them or Google applies its account and storage policies. You can download a copy in the app, manage the files in Drive, and revoke Sayarathy's access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Disconnecting in the app clears its current token but does not revoke Google's permission grant. The app's Delete action permanently deletes the selected file after confirmation.

Google API data use

Sayarathy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the file storage features you request. It is not sold or used for advertising or AI-model training.
