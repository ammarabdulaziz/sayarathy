Sayarathy

Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Sayarathy is a browser application operated by Ammar Abdul Aziz for keeping car-maintenance records and attachments in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

When you connect Google Drive, Google asks you to authorize the drive.file permission. Sayarathy uses it to create, read, update, list, and manage the files created by or explicitly opened with the app. It does not grant access to every file in your Drive.

Where your data goes

Your vehicle profile, services, schedules, mileage, notes, follow-ups, and attachment references are stored in a private JSON file in your Google account. Receipts and photos are uploaded as separate files in an app-created Drive folder. Content travels directly between your browser and Google Drive over HTTPS. Sayarathy does not send those records to an operator-owned backend, analytics service, advertising service, or AI service.

Browser memory, preferences, and installation

Google access tokens, the open log, uploaded-file previews, form edits, and retryable drafts are held in browser memory. Vehicle records and tokens are not deliberately persisted in browser storage. The app stores only an opaque preferred Drive file ID in localStorage, associated with the public OAuth client ID, to reopen your selected log after login. That ID must appear in the currently authorized account's app-file list before it is used. Clearing browser data removes this preference but does not delete your Drive files.

The installable PWA uses a network-only service worker. It does not cache your log, provide an offline mode, or queue changes for background synchronization. When the app is open and visible, it checks Drive for updates after login, when you return to it, when connectivity returns, and periodically. It pauses automatic refreshes while you edit or have unsaved changes.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts application and information pages and may process ordinary web request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy policies. Google Identity Services provides authorization, and Google Drive stores your files under Google's privacy policies. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

Retention, deletion, and control

Drive files remain until you delete them or Google applies its account/storage policies. Deleting a service or removing an attachment in Sayarathy removes the JSON reference, not the physical receipt or photo. This preserves references in older backups; delete unwanted physical files in Google Drive. The app attempts to remove partial uploads when an upload operation fails. You can export a JSON backup, download attachments, and revoke the app's access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Disconnecting in the app clears local authorization but does not revoke the Google permission grant.

Google API data use

Sayarathy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you request. It is not sold or used for advertising or AI-model training.
